Q&A

Q: Where does your project name come from?

A: It is a reference to the Blackened Order of Denarius from the urban fantasy book series The Dresden Files, which I am a big fan of.

Q: What equipment do you use?

A: I use the DAW Reaper with the plugins Vital for synths and drum machines, Spitfire Labs / Splice Instrument for presets of "real" instruments like guitars and piano, and ReaPack for various effects like distortion and pitch-shifting. I do not play or own any instruments.

Q: Will you release your music on streaming services?

A: One day, but not now. Uploading music to streaming is actually pretty cheap, but I'm unemployed, so I don't have a constant stream of revenue to pay for it. If my music gets any attention (doubt it), I might do it.
